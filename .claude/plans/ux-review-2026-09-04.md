# OhMyReads — UX/UI review (2026-09-04)

Senior UX/UI review of production (`https://ohmyreads-next.vercel.app`, deploy of `2d192b7`).
Two tracks: a live walk-through on desktop (1280×800) and mobile (390×844), signed out and
signed in with a throwaway account (created and deleted the same day), plus a code-level
design review by the `ui-ux-design-reviewer` agent. Nothing was implemented.
Benchmarks: Goodreads, StoryGraph, Fable, Literal.

Severity: **Critical** = broken or blocks a task · **High** = clear friction or an accessibility
failure · **Medium** = polish · **Low** = nice-to-have.

## Overall assessment

The product has a real point of view: warm literary palette, serif headings, privacy-aware
profiles, the Open Library vs local rating split, a community map. The chrome that was hardened
recently (command palette, More sheet, skip link, reduced motion, admin surfaces) is at market
level. The depth is uneven below that: the three controls readers touch most (shelf dropdown,
progress dialog, Mood Search dialog) are first-draft DOM with decorative ARIA, mobile layout does
not reserve space for its own fixed chrome, and the feed queries lose covers. Four consolidations
(one dialog primitive, one menu primitive, one nav manifest, one contrast token) plus the mobile
padding fix resolve most of the top ten and leave the design system stronger than twenty patches
would. The catalog also needs a data pass before wider testing: a tester's first impression is
formed by the Harry Potter record and the blank covers, not by the code.
